Artist profile

Oaxaca as her roots. Europe as her horizon.

Naomi Fernanda Villegas Luna, known on stage as Naomi Luna, is a singer from Oaxaca with Mixtec roots. Her voice, a mixed tessitura, is at home in the bolero and draws on jazz, soul and bel canto technique.

She trained from childhood between Oaxaca and Mexico City and continued her studies in Lyon, France. During her years in Prague she sang at official events of the Embassy of Mexico, from Independence Day to diplomatic receptions, and brought Oaxacan music to festivals and cultural organizations. Back in Oaxaca she founded the ensemble Corazón Antequera, and today she sings at weddings, private dinners, hotels and cultural events.

Music as a territory of memory, identity and community.

Journey

A career across two continents

From today back to where it began: the stages, institutions and classical training behind her voice.

  1. TodayOaxaca

    Back home

    She founds Corazón Antequera, a quintet of Oaxacan musicians reimagining traditional music from the state through jazz, bossa nova and Latin American rhythms. She is also part of the stage project Oaxaca Vive, touring internationally, mainly in the United States and Latin America.

  2. 2019–2025Prague · Czech Republic

    Featured collaboration

    With the Embassy of Mexico in the Czech Republic

    During her years in Prague, Naomi worked with the Embassy of Mexico, singing at its official ceremonies and celebrations: Independence Day commemorations, national anniversaries, private diplomatic receptions and events open to the public. Her voice accompanied diplomats, guests of honor and the Mexican community in Europe.

    2019
    Honorable mention

    Awarded by the Embassy of Mexico for the events commemorating the 209th anniversary of Mexican Independence.

  3. 2020–2025Cultural festivals

    Mexico alive in Europe

    She performs regularly at La Calle Mexicana and at the Day of the Dead Festival of the Náprstek Museum of Asian, African and American Cultures, promoting Mexican and Oaxacan music with cultural festivals and non-profit organizations.

  4. 2020–2024Central and Eastern Europe

    Touring with the choir

    With the Charles University choir she performs across the Czech Republic and in other Eastern European countries.

  5. 2019Prague, Czech Republic

    Arriving in Prague

    She joins the Charles University choir. That same year, Club México CZ recognizes her musical contribution to the Sixth Congress of Mexican Associations in Europe.

  6. Age 18Lyon, France

    Training in Europe

    She arrives in Lyon on a cultural exchange, in a program that prepares students to enter the Lyon Regional Conservatory. Her main vocal coach is soprano Isabel Germain.

  7. 2014Oaxaca

    First leading role

    At the CEDART Miguel Cabrera arts high school she builds her stage training. At 17 she sings the title role in Mozart’s opera Bastien and Bastienne, produced by the Instituto de Formación Musical Maurice Ravel.

  8. Age 8Oaxaca · Ciudad de México

    Where the voice began

    She begins her musical training at CIMO and Esperanza Azteca. She studies bel canto with Felipe Espinoza and Gabriel Mijares in Mexico City, and with Verónica Murúa, faculty member at the UNAM School of Music.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Which experience is right for me?

As a guide: Ceremony (duo or trio) for intimate ceremonies; Cocktail & dinner (trio or quintet) for receptions and dinners; The full experience (quintet) for weddings and galas with music from start to finish. Tell us about the venue and guest count and we will recommend the best option.

How much does it cost?

Every quote is tailored to the date, venue, length and format. Send us your event details and we will send you a proposal.

Do you sing in English and French?

Yes. The repertoire includes songs in Spanish, English, French and Italian, ideal for destination weddings and events with international guests.

Can we request a song that is not on the list?

The published repertoire is a selection. Tell us which song you have in mind and we will review it with the ensemble.

Do you perform throughout the state of Oaxaca?

Yes. We are based in Oaxaca City and perform throughout the state: the Central Valleys, the coast (Huatulco, Puerto Escondido and Mazunte), the Sierra Norte, the Mixteca and the Isthmus. For events outside the city, travel is quoted according to the location; write to us with the date and venue.

Vocals and direction


Naomi Luna


Bolero, jazz and soul with bel canto training.


Oaxacan singer with Mixtec roots: bolero, jazz and soul shaped by bel canto technique.


She began training at the age of eight at CIMO and Esperanza Azteca and studied bel canto with Felipe Espinoza, Gabriel Mijares and Verónica Murúa. She continued at CEDART “Miguel Cabrera” in Oaxaca and in Lyon, France, with soprano Isabel Germain. In Prague she sang at official events of the Embassy of Mexico and with the Charles University choir; back in Oaxaca she founded Corazón Antequera.

Bass and double bass


Canek León


From symphony orchestra to jazz: Bellas Artes, Cervantino and Camerata Oaxaca.


Classically trained double bassist from Oaxaca, whose career runs from the symphony orchestra to chamber music and jazz.


Born in Oaxaca, he built his musical path from high school at CEDART “Miguel Cabrera” and went on to study double bass performance at the Escuela Superior de Música. He played with the Orquesta Escuela Carlos Chávez from 2017 until completing his studies there in 2024, has performed with the ESM Orchestra and the Oaxaca Symphony Orchestra, and is an active member of the Camerata Oaxaca.


His versatility reaches beyond the classical double bass: he has played in jazz and chamber ensembles on the national scene, with a repertoire spanning orchestral music, jazz, tango and rock.
